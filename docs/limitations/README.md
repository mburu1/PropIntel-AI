# Limitations and Risks

- Data quality limits the reliability of forecasts and recommendations.
- OCR and extraction can misread poor scans, tables, handwriting, or jurisdiction-specific language.
- AI can hallucinate or follow malicious instructions in documents; citations and approval do not eliminate review.
- Provider outages can delay payments, notifications, maps, or model responses.
- Regional tenancy, privacy, tax, accounting, and payment rules require local validation.
- Portfolio-scale analytics may be eventually consistent.
- A modular monolith still needs disciplined ownership to avoid accidental coupling.
- Cost can grow with document volume, model tokens, storage, and notification traffic.

Each limitation needs an owner, a measurable detection signal, and a mitigation before the affected capability is marketed as autonomous.
