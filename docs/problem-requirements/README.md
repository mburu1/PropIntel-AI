# Problem and Requirements

## Problem

Property operations are fragmented across spreadsheets, email, messaging apps, accounting tools, document folders, and vendor portals. This creates duplicate records, slow response times, missed lease and maintenance obligations, weak financial visibility, and decisions based on stale or incomplete information.

## Outcomes

- Reduce time to answer portfolio and resident questions.
- Increase on-time maintenance, lease, and payment workflows.
- Give owners and operators a trustworthy view of occupancy, income, costs, risk, and exceptions.
- Make documents searchable and convert them into reviewable structured facts.
- Preserve human control over financial, legal, and resident-impacting decisions.

## Functional requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| FR-01 | Manage organizations, properties, buildings, units, and occupancy. |
| FR-02 | Manage parties, roles, leases, applications, renewals, and notices. |
| FR-03 | Capture resident conversations and maintenance requests with status and SLA. |
| FR-04 | Create, assign, schedule, and close work orders with vendor and cost tracking. |
| FR-05 | Ingest, classify, extract, version, search, and retain documents. |
| FR-06 | Generate charges and invoices, accept payments, issue refunds, and reconcile provider results. |
| FR-07 | Provide dashboards, exports, alerts, forecasts, anomalies, and recommendations. |
| FR-08 | Support grounded AI chat and actions with citations, confidence, approval, and audit. |
| FR-09 | Integrate identity, payment, messaging, accounting, maps, and document providers. |
| FR-10 | Enforce organization and property-level authorization and maintain an audit trail. |

## Quality requirements

Security, availability, performance, observability, accessibility, data retention, disaster recovery, and testability requirements are defined in their dedicated documents.

## Explicit exclusions for the first release

Mortgage origination, property construction management, autonomous legal advice, autonomous eviction decisions, custody of payment funds, and a general-purpose consumer marketplace are out of scope.
